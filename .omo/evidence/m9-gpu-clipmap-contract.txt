M9 GPU clipmap contract (2026-09-05)

Implementation: src/render/gi/gpu.rs; shader: assets/shaders/gi_gpu.wgsl.
try_new(adapter) rejects unsupported Rgba8Unorm 3D/storage or <128 dimensions
before publishing resources, then allocates four material + four light
128^3 Rgba8Unorm 3D textures transactionally. Clipmap origins and ring offsets
are copied into a persistent uniform. upload() splits each z slab so every
queue.write_texture payload is <= 4 MiB. record() dispatches trace, temporal,
and denoise at quarter resolution with workgroups of 8x8; user-off/fallback
sets enabled=false and records zero dispatches.

Verification invocation:
  cargo fmt --all && cargo check --release

  Finished `release` profile; source line-count maximum 490 (runtime_effects.rs).
Renderer integration:
  Renderer owns Option<GpuGi>, configure_gpu_gi(adapter,width,height) performs
  one-time transactional initialization/fallback, every frame updates logical
  clipmap origin/ring state and records the real compute trace/temporal/1-2-4
  denoise dispatches. configure_gi(false) maps to DisabledByUser and produces
  zero GPU GI dispatches. gi_clipmap_memory_bytes and gi_dispatch_count expose
  live benchmark counters.
